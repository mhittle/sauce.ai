## Your clients' doctors are vibe-coding.

In a Sept. 22, 2026 MGMA Stat poll, about **34%** of medical group leaders said their practice has built its own app or workflow tool in-house using AI. Another **8%** said they tried but did not launch. The poll had **228 applicable responses**.
Source: MGMA Stat, "Would you DIY an EHR with AI?", published 9/23/2026: https://www.mgma.com/mgma-stat/would-you-vibe-code-an-ehr-with-ai

MGMA's own guidance in the same piece: "A homegrown tool that handles ePHI belongs in your practice's HIPAA security risk analysis (SRA)." It also says: "Do not assume a consumer AI account includes a BAA; verify the product, account tier, and terms before using it with PHI."

In practice, a physician builds an intake form, a referral tracker or a patient-messaging tool on Lovable, Replit, Base44, Bubble, Cursor or Claude Code. It works, and staff start to rely on it. Often nobody in the practice knows which vendors it touches, whether those vendors sign a business associate agreement (BAA), or who owns the tool if it breaks. Those questions usually reach you, the practice's IT or compliance partner, last.
